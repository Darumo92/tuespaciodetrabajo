Desk height is not a fixed magic number. It is the height that lets your shoulders relax, your elbows stay close to your body and your wrists remain neutral while typing.

## The quick setup

1. Set your chair so your feet are flat and knees are comfortable.
2. Sit back against the backrest.
3. Bring the keyboard close.
4. Adjust desk or chair height until your elbows are around 90 degrees.
5. Raise the monitor so you are not looking down.

If the desk is fixed and too high, do not hunch your shoulders to reach it. Raise the chair and use a footrest. If the desk is too low, a riser is rarely elegant; a different desk or standing desk may be the cleaner fix.

## The common mistake

Many people set the chair for the desk instead of their body. That is backwards. Your body comes first, then the furniture. In my own setup, the chair height is the anchor; the desk and monitor follow.

## Laptop warning

A laptop on the desk forces a compromise: either the keyboard is comfortable and the screen is too low, or the screen is high and the keyboard is unusable. Use a laptop stand plus external keyboard, or connect a monitor. The [home office ergonomics guide](/en/guides/how-to-set-up-an-ergonomic-home-office/) covers that full setup.
