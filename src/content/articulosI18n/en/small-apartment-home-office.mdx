A small apartment home office has one advantage: it forces you to be honest. If a product does not solve a real problem, it has no place. The goal is not to recreate a showroom desk; it is to build a compact setup that does not punish your back, eyes or focus.

## Choose the desk by movement space

People measure wall width and forget chair depth. You need room to pull the chair back and stand up. A 120 x 60 cm desk is often better than a wider desk that blocks the room.

## Use vertical space

Wall shelves, pegboards and monitor arms free surface area. A monitor arm is especially useful because it removes the original stand footprint and lets the keyboard sit closer.

## Keep the setup reversible

If you rent, avoid drilling unless you have permission. Clamp lights, cable trays, freestanding shelves and monitor arms give you flexibility without turning a small room into a permanent workshop.
