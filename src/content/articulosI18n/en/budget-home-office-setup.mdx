A budget home office should not feel cheap in the places your body touches all day. Spend first on the parts that affect posture: chair, screen height and desk size. Everything else can wait.

## The buying order

1. Chair with real lumbar support.
2. Desk large enough for your screen and keyboard.
3. Monitor or laptop stand so the screen reaches eye level.
4. External keyboard and mouse.
5. Task lighting.
6. Cable management and comfort extras.

That order is not glamorous, but it works. My first remote-work setup was modest, and the mistakes were predictable: I cared too much about how the desk looked and too little about the chair.

## Where not to overspend

Do not buy premium headphones, decorative panels, plants, desk mats and smart gadgets before you have a chair that supports you. Do not buy a giant monitor if your desk is too shallow. Do not buy a standing desk if the remaining budget leaves you on a bad chair.
