Screen eye strain is not just "too much screen". It is usually bad contrast: a bright monitor in a dim room, glare from a window, tiny text, or a laptop screen that keeps your head and eyes working harder than they need to.

## Match the screen to the room

Your screen should not feel like a lamp. If the room is dark, lower brightness or improve ambient light. If the room is bright, reduce glare before simply pushing brightness higher.

## Fix distance and text size

Place the monitor roughly an arm's length away and increase text scaling if you squint. A sharp 27-inch monitor can make reading much easier, but only if it is at the right height and distance.

## Use breaks that are easy to remember

The 20-20-20 rule is boring because it works. Look away regularly, blink consciously and stand up when you can. If your eyes burn every day despite changes, schedule an eye exam.
