My home office is not a showroom. It is a small room in Rubi where I work, write, take calls and occasionally fight cable clutter like everyone else. The setup has evolved over years, and every component earned its place by solving a real discomfort.

## The core setup

The chair is the foundation. I use an ergonomic mesh chair because lower-back support matters more than any desk accessory. The standing desk lets me change position during the day, but I do not stand all morning. The monitor is a 27-inch 4K USB-C model because it gives me one cable, sharp text and a proper eye-level screen.

## Keyboard, mouse and light

The ergonomic keyboard and vertical mouse were not aesthetic decisions. They were wrist decisions. The first week with the keyboard felt odd; now a flat laptop keyboard feels like the compromise.

## What I would change

I would choose a wider desktop, improve the room acoustics and add better storage for the laptop. The lesson is simple: a setup is never finished, but it should keep getting easier to use.
