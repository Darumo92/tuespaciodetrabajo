Cable management is not about making a desk look perfect for photos. It is about making the desk easier to clean, easier to move and less annoying every time you plug in a laptop.

## Start under the desk

Mount or place a cable tray under the rear edge. Put the power strip there, then run monitor, lamp and charger cables into it. The goal is one clean cable going to the wall.

## Use velcro, not permanent fixes

Velcro ties beat zip ties because you will change the setup. New monitor, different laptop, extra lamp: the desk evolves. Keep the system reversible.

## Standing desk warning

Raise the desk to maximum height before finalising cables. If a cable is tight at the top, it will eventually pull, unplug or break.
