Good workspace lighting is about balance. You want enough light on the desk to read, type and write without glare, but you do not want the monitor fighting a dark room or a lamp shining into the screen.

## Match light to the screen

If the room is dim, lower monitor brightness or add task light. If the room is bright, control window glare first. A lamp cannot fix a screen facing direct sun.

## Choose adjustable light

I prefer lamps with dimming and several colour temperatures. Use neutral light for focus and warmer light when the day winds down. A single harsh cold setting gets tiring quickly.

## Avoid reflections

Place lamps to the side, not behind the monitor or pointed at the panel. Monitor light bars are useful because they illuminate the desk surface without hitting the screen directly.
