Carpal tunnel prevention is about reducing repeated stress before it becomes a daily symptom. Remote work can make the problem worse because the desk is often improvised and breaks disappear into back-to-back calls.

## Watch the early signs

Tingling in the thumb, index or middle finger, wrist ache, forearm tightness and a hand that feels clumsy late in the day are signals to act. Do not wait until pain becomes the way you know the workday is ending.

## Fix mouse and keyboard position

Keep the mouse close so the shoulder is not reaching. Consider a vertical mouse if a flat mouse leaves your wrist tense. Keep the keyboard flat or slightly negative, not propped up at the back.

## Move before symptoms appear

Open and close your hands, stretch the forearms gently and stand up regularly. Small breaks work better than a long routine you skip.
