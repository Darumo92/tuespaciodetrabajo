Working from home in hot weather is mostly about timing. Once the room has absorbed heat, every fix gets harder. The best strategy starts before the room feels hot.

## Morning routine

Ventilate early if outside air is cooler. Close blinds before direct sun hits. Keep the door open if the rest of the home is cooler, and close it only when calls or noise require it.

## Reduce heat sources

Monitors, laptops, chargers and lamps all add heat. Use only the screens you need, keep chargers off fabric and switch to efficient LED lighting.

## Use airflow intelligently

A quiet fan helps you feel cooler without fighting the microphone. Place it to the side, use oscillation and avoid blasting air directly at your face during calls.
