Back pain from working at home is rarely caused by one object. It is usually the combination of a weak chair, a desk at the wrong height and long blocks without movement.

## Start with the chair

Sit all the way back. If your lower back is not supported, you will slide forward and round the spine. That is why I would rather buy a decent chair before a fancy desk. If your chair has lumbar support, adjust it. If it does not, consider upgrading or using a firm temporary cushion.

## Check desk height

If the desk is too high, your shoulders rise. If it is too low, you collapse forward. Both can show up as back pain. Use the [correct height guide](/en/guides/correct-desk-and-chair-height/) to match chair and desk properly.

## Add movement

Movement beats perfect posture. Stand for calls, walk between tasks and reset your position every hour. A standing desk helps only if you use it as a movement tool, not as a badge of productivity.
