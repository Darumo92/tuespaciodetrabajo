A standing desk is worth it when it changes your behaviour. The motor, memory presets and stable frame are not the benefit themselves; they make movement easier. That is what helps.

## What a standing desk does well

It breaks long sitting blocks. It gives you a natural way to change energy after lunch. It can make short calls or reading tasks more active. It also helps people who feel stiff after staying in one position too long.

## What it does not solve

Standing badly is still bad posture. If your screen is low, your wrists are bent or your shoes are uncomfortable, standing will not magically fix the setup. I would not buy a standing desk before solving the chair and monitor height.

## Who should buy one

Buy one if you work full days at a desk, can afford a stable model and like the idea of alternating positions. Skip it if you only work from home occasionally or your budget would force you to keep a poor chair.
