A complete home office is not a shopping list of expensive gear. It is a system: chair, desk, screen, input devices, lighting and enough order that you can start work without fighting the room.

## Priority order

Start with the body. A chair that supports your back matters more than decorative accessories. Then make sure the desk is stable and large enough. After that, bring the screen to eye level with a monitor or laptop stand. Finally, tune lighting and cables.

My own setup took years to build. The useful lesson is not the exact products; it is the order. I solved the pain points first: chair, monitor height, keyboard and mouse, then lighting.

## The essentials

- Chair with useful lumbar support.
- Desk with enough width and depth for your screen and keyboard.
- Monitor or laptop stand at eye level.
- External keyboard and mouse.
- Neutral task light for late afternoons.
- Cable control good enough that cleaning the desk is easy.

## Do not buy everything at once

Use the setup for two weeks before upgrading the next thing. If your neck hurts, improve screen height. If your lower back hurts, look at the chair. If your eyes are tired, check lighting and brightness.
