Soundproofing a home office without renovation is mostly about expectations. You can make calls clearer, reduce echo and soften household noise. You cannot turn a rented room into a recording studio with thin foam squares.

## Block vs absorb

Blocking noise requires mass and sealing. Absorbing sound reduces reflections inside the room. Acoustic panels help with echo; they do not magically stop traffic, neighbours or a washing machine behind the wall.

## Start with gaps

Door gaps leak sound. A draft stopper and adhesive seals are cheap and often more effective than decorative foam. Curtains and rugs help because they add soft surfaces where bare rooms would reflect sound.

## Improve calls

Move the microphone closer to your mouth, use noise suppression and avoid placing the desk in the most echo-heavy corner. Sometimes the best acoustic upgrade is a better microphone position, not more panels.
